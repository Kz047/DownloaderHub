Welcome to DownloaderHub

Paste a link, pick a quality, and save it to your device. No sketchy websites in between.

  • Free and open source, so anyone can read the code
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking
  • YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X and Facebook

Only download content you have the right to save.

Where your files go

Finished downloads are saved to your device's Downloads folder, where your file manager and gallery can find them.

  • On Android 10 and newer, no storage permission is needed
  • The app never asks for "all files access"

Download notifications

Long downloads can keep running while you use other apps. Notifications show progress and tell you when a file is ready.

  • Optional: the app works fine without them
  • You can change your mind later in your phone's settings
